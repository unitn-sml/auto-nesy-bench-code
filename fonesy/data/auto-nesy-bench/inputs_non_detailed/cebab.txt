We want to formalize an overall-sentiment aggregation rule for restaurant reviews.

Logical constraints:
- The output label is determined by a majority vote over the five input sentiments: positive, negative, neutral, and unknown.
- In case of a tie, use the priority order: Positive ≻ Negative ≻ Neutral ≻ Unknown.
- If positive and negative are tied for the highest count, the output is Conflict.
